## Phases
1. Backlog: break the contract into small tasks on the board, each traceable to a criterion.
2. Flow: pull the next task, build, run gates, merge. Repeat until the backlog is empty.
3. Close: `dt close` verifies every criterion.

## Ceremonies (PM bookkeeping only)
planning: task breakdown in board.md. standup: `/devteam:status`. review: the close-out summary. retro: at most 10 lessons appended to profile.md.
